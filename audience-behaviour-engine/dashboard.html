5 · What should the marketing team do next?

Each recommendation is generated from the numbers above and shows the evidence that triggered it. Priority reflects how many customers it affects and how serious the issue is.

High  Customer service: Fix late-delivery complaints; reply on storm posts first

Evidence: 9% of comments are delivery complaints. Hot spots: Brand C (15%, 1.7x), 11.11 Sale (16%, 1.8x), 12.12 Sale (18%, 2.0x). 29 posts are complaint storms (far more complaints than comparable posts).

Action: Assign support to reply within 2 hours on storm posts, pin a delivery-timeline note on sale posts, and review courier performance for the hot-spot brands/campaigns.

Track: Delivery-complaint share; first-reply time

High  Promotions: Answer discount requests before they are asked

Evidence: 10% of comments ask about discounts, codes or sales. Highest in 11.11 Sale (1.3x average). Most of them comment on Sale Announcements (42%).

Action: Put the offer or code in the caption and pinned comment of sale posts, and test a bundle offer against a flat percentage discount.

Track: Discount-request share; conversion on promo code

High  Content: Instagram: lead with Eid Campaigns, keep Product Photo, Sale Announcement for conversion

Evidence: Median engagement index 1.56 for Eid Campaign (90% CI 1.40-1.63) vs 0.66 for Sale Announcement (113 vs 198 posts). Evidence: strong (intervals do not overlap).

Action: Use Eid Campaigns for reach, but do not drop Product Photo, Sale Announcement: they get less attention yet carry more buying-signal comments.

Track: Engagement index per post; buying-signal rate

Medium  Product & stock: Close stock and city-availability gaps

Evidence: 11% of comments ask if the item is available. Most-named cities: Karachi (21%), Lahore (20%), Islamabad (13%). Brands above average: Brand D (18%).

Action: Show 'in stock / sold out' and city availability on posts; restock the sizes and cities named most; consider a waitlist or pre-order.

Track: Availability-enquiry share

Medium  Content: Product Photos convert better than they attract

Evidence: Buying-signal comments are 49% of comments (all posts: 48%) but engagement index is only 0.69.

Action: Give Product Photos paid reach or influencer cross-posting; they reach fewer people but the ones reached want to buy.

Track: Buying-signal comments per 1,000 likes

Medium  Content: Sale Announcements convert better than they attract

Evidence: Buying-signal comments are 67% of comments (all posts: 48%) but engagement index is only 0.64.

Action: Give Sale Announcements paid reach or influencer cross-posting; they reach fewer people but the ones reached want to buy.

Track: Buying-signal comments per 1,000 likes

Low  Content: TikTok: lead with Reels, cut back Influencer Posts

Evidence: Median engagement index 1.29 for Reel (90% CI 1.17-1.43) vs 0.95 for Influencer Post (269 vs 174 posts). Evidence: strong (intervals do not overlap).

Action: Shift about 15-20% of TikTok posting slots from Influencer Posts (low attention and few buying signals) to Reels for the next campaign and re-measure.

Track: Engagement index per post; buying-signal rate

Low  Content: Facebook: lead with Eid Campaigns, keep Sale Announcement, Product Photo for conversion

Evidence: Median engagement index 1.78 for Eid Campaign (90% CI 1.50-2.09) vs 0.66 for Product Photo (48 vs 166 posts). Evidence: strong (intervals do not overlap).

Action: Use Eid Campaigns for reach, but do not drop Sale Announcement, Product Photo: they get less attention yet carry more buying-signal comments.

Track: Engagement index per post; buying-signal rate

Low  Content: Reels get attention but little buying intent

Evidence: Engagement index 1.36 (above typical) but only 39% of comments show buying signals.

Action: Add a clear call to action on Reels (price in caption, 'DM to order', shop link in bio).

Track: Buying-signal rate

Low  Content: Influencer Posts get attention but little buying intent

Evidence: Engagement index 1.07 (above typical) but only 42% of comments show buying signals.

Action: Add a clear call to action on Influencer Posts (price in caption, 'DM to order', shop link in bio).

Track: Buying-signal rate

Low  Content: Styling Videos get attention but little buying intent

Evidence: Engagement index 1.07 (above typical) but only 42% of comments show buying signals.

Action: Add a clear call to action on Styling Videos (price in caption, 'DM to order', shop link in bio).

Track: Buying-signal rate

Low  Campaign testing: Run three A/B tests next campaign

Evidence: Price in caption vs no price on Product Photos (13% of Product Photo comments just ask 'price?'); 'DM to order' / shop-link call to action vs none on Reels (Reels attract praise but fewer buying signals than Sale Announcements); Offer code in caption vs offer in image only on Sale Announcements (Discount requests are the largest buying-signal group on sale posts).

Action: To detect a 25% lift in engagement you need about 79 posts per arm (log-engagement spread 0.50); for smaller teams, run each test across 3-4 weeks and several brands/platforms.

Track: Price-enquiry share, buying-signal rate; Buying-signal comments per 1,000 likes; Discount-request share, promo-code redemptions

Low  Promotions: Reframe value where price pushback is high

Evidence: 5% of comments say the price is too high. Strongest: Facebook (8%, 1.6x), Brand E (8%, 1.4x).

Action: Test value framing (fabric details, cost per wear, instalment or bundle pricing) against the current price-only caption.

Track: Price-complaint share

Low  Customer service: Filter spam and fake-giveaway comments

Evidence: 4% of comments are spam/promotion, 41% of them on TikTok; 31 posts are spam-flooded.

Action: Turn on keyword filters (giveaway, follow, replica, DM for) and hide flagged comments so they do not bury real buyer questions.

Track: Spam share of comments

Low  Customer service: Publish delivery times and charges up front

Evidence: 5% of comments ask how long delivery takes, what it costs or whether it arrives before Eid.

Action: Add delivery days by city and the cash-on-delivery option to captions and highlights.

Track: Delivery-enquiry share
